Bill Panwei Hu

I study quantitative finance at NYU. I like problems where a clean piece of mathematics has to survive contact with real market data — and I read philosophy on the side, which turns out to be the same habit applied to harder-to-falsify material.

Most of what I build falls into three groups: derivatives pricing, where the question is whether a PDE can be solved fast enough and without arbitrage; microstructure and strategy, where the question is whether an edge survives fees, latency and your own overfitting; and tools, which exist because I wanted them to.

The notes section is separate and unapologetic. It is mostly Hegel.

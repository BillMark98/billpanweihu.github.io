How reason built itself by excluding madness

Reading notes on Foucault's History of Madness — not a history of mental illness, but a history of the boundary, and of who got to draw it.

On this page

  • 一、核心问题意识

  • 三、「治疗」不等于进步
  • What to do with this

The mistake that makes History of Madness unreadable is opening it expecting a history of psychiatry — a story in which we gradually stopped chaining people up and started treating them. Foucault is not telling that story, and he does not think it is true.

His question is not what is madness? It is: how did madness come to be seen, named, confined, and governed — and who acquired the authority to say that this is what it was?

Put differently: the book is about reason, not madness. Its claim is that reason constitutes itself by drawing a line and putting something on the other side of it.

一、核心问题意识

在读任何一章之前，先记住这一点：

福柯不是在写「精神疾病史」，而是在写：理性如何通过排除疯癫来建构自身。

三组对立贯穿全书：

  • 理性 / 非理性
  • 正常 / 异常
  • 话语 / 权力

关键不在于疯癫「是什么」，而在于它如何被看见、被命名、被隔离、被治理——以及谁有权说它是疯癫。

The three ages

The book is not chronological narrative but a genealogy, in which the same boundary is redrawn three times.

The Renaissance. Madness is in the world, not outside it. It sits alongside the poet, the prophet and the fool; it has a relation to truth, to death, to the cosmos. It is something that can be heard. The ship of fools is a real practice and also an image of this: madness circulates, it is not fixed in place.

The Classical Age — the Great Confinement. In the mid-seventeenth century, across Europe, the mad are locked up. What matters for Foucault's argument is who they are locked up with: the poor, the idle, the unemployed, the venereal, the blasphemous. The criterion is not medical. It is the failure to work and the failure to conform — unreason, not illness. Confinement is a moral and economic operation before it is a clinical one.

The Modern Age — the birth of the asylum. Chains come off; Pinel and Tuke are celebrated as liberators. Foucault's reading is the one that makes people angry: this is not liberation, it is a change of technique.

三、「治疗」不等于进步

⚠️ 这是全书最容易误读的地方：

在福柯那里，「治疗」≠ 进步，而是权力技术的升级。

旧的约束是外在的：锁链、围墙、身体的禁闭。新的约束是内在化的：病人被要求自我监视、自我评判、自我矫正。医生的权威不再依靠体力，而依靠凝视（le regard）与道德判断。

枷锁被取下，是因为已经不需要了——更有效的约束已经搬进了病人自己的意识里。

What to do with this

Two things are worth separating.

The historiography is contested. Specialist historians have disputed Foucault's dating, his sources, and the scale of the confinement he describes. Those objections are serious and a reader should know they exist.

The analytical move survives them. Foucault's durable contribution is the reframing: asking not what a category contains but what work the category does, and for whom. Applied to madness it produces this book. Applied to criminality it produces Discipline and Punish. The method outruns the particular case.

The uncomfortable implication is the one about our own moment. We also have a boundary. We also believe ours is drawn by evidence rather than by interest. Every age has believed that.

Ulysses, or an ordinary day at epic scale

Notes on reading Joyce against Homer — where the correspondence helps, where it misleads, and why so little happens on purpose.

Reading Ulysses as "the Odyssey relocated to Dublin" undersells it. Never having read the Odyssey at all means missing a structural layer Joyce deliberately built. Both are avoidable.

The better framing is as an experiment:

Homer needed ten years, the whole Mediterranean, gods, monsters, war and adventure to bring one hero home. Joyce uses one day, one city, and a few ordinary people eating, defecating, working, wanting, envying, grieving — to ask whether modern daily life is itself epic material.

The plot, in full

16 June 1904, Dublin, about eighteen hours.

Stephen Dedalus leaves his lodging, teaches a class, wanders the city.

Leopold Bloom makes his wife breakfast, goes out, attends a funeral, visits a newspaper office, eats lunch, passes the library, sits in a pub, walks on the beach, calls at a maternity hospital, ends up in the red-light district, meets Stephen, brings him home, watches him leave, goes to bed.

Molly Bloom lies in bed and thinks.

That is the book.

如果你按传统小说「发生了什么」来读，你会不断产生一个反应：

就这？？

恰恰如此。 这个反应是被设计出来的。

The correspondence is ironic, not decorative

Joyce did map eighteen episodes onto Homer, and did assign each a time, an organ, an art, a symbol and a narrative technique — the Linati and Gilbert schemas circulated among friends. But the mapping is almost never straight. It is ironic, inverted, deflationary, sometimes deliberately misaligned.

OdysseusBloom
Blinds the CyclopsArgues with a nationalist drunk in a pub
Resists the SirensListens to music in a bar, thinks about women
Returns to Ithaca after ten yearsGets home to 7 Eccles Street, having forgotten his key

The gap between the two columns is where the meaning is. Bloom is not a diminished Odysseus; the comparison asks whether the heroic frame was ever the right measure of a life.

五条主线

读这本书，与其追情节，不如抓五条线：

① 日常生活的史诗化 — 表层事件极少，但人物脑中的记忆、语言、欲望、广告、政治、宗教、音乐、历史不断涌入。史诗的规模被转移到意识内部。

② 父与子 — Stephen 失去（或拒绝）了父亲，Bloom 失去了儿子 Rudy。两人整日在同一座城市中错身而过，最终相遇——而相遇之后，什么也没有解决。

③ 流亡与不属于 — Stephen 住在自己的塔里却不属于它；Bloom 是都柏林人，却因犹太身份始终是外人。

④ 身体 — Joyce 坚持写吃、排泄、月经、手淫、腐烂。这不是挑衅，而是论点的一部分：若日常生活配得上史诗，那就必须整个地配得上。

⑤ 语言本身成为主角 — 每一章换一种写法。到了后半部，文体不再是容器，而是内容。

Where the difficulty actually is

Not in the allusions. Those have annotations, and looking them up is mechanical.

The difficulty is that the narrative technique changes under you. An episode written as a newspaper, an episode as a catechism, an episode as a parody sequence running through the history of English prose, an episode as a hallucinated play. Each demands a different reading posture, and the book does not announce the switch.

The practical consequence: do not read it at a constant speed, and do not read it only once. Episode 1 rewards rereading after episode 18 in a way that very few novels do.

开头那一句

"Stately, plump Buck Mulligan came from the stairhead…"

Mulligan 端着刮胡碗走上塔顶，戏仿弥撒。一个亵渎的仪式，由一个不信的人表演给另一个不信的人看——而后者恰恰因为拒绝在母亲临终前祷告而备受折磨。

爱尔兰、天主教、殖民、羞耻、艺术家的自我放逐——全书最重的几个主题，在第一页就已经全部到齐。
